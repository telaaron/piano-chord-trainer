Questions, answered

What is a jazz piano voicing?
A voicing is a specific way of arranging a chord's notes on the keyboard. The same B♭maj7 can be played as a three-note shell, a rootless left-hand shape, or a wide two-handed spread — same chord, entirely different sound.
Do I need to know jazz already?
No. The first course starts at intervals and seventh chords. If you can find middle C, you can start today.
Can I practice with a MIDI keyboard?
Yes — connect it over USB or Bluetooth and the app checks every chord you play automatically. A microphone or the on-screen keys work too.
How much time do I need?
Five minutes a day beats an hour on Sunday. A coach session runs 5–15 minutes depending on how much time you have.
Is it free?
All four courses, all nine voicing types and every progression in twelve keys are free. Pro adds adaptive difficulty, custom progressions and cloud sync.
